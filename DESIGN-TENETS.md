# The quality gate above the design core

Rams and NYCTA define this system's grammar. The Bauhaus-informed method adds
evidence that a particular composition serves a real person and real content.
It does not extend the closed token palette, invent an identity colour, soften
the geometry or override the manuals and their decision trees.

## Apply without changing the grammar

- State the person, context, intended outcome and obstacle before drawing.
- Inventory earned content and controls. Compare two genuinely different
  information arrangements using the same real material.
- Explain what each plane, number, gap and alignment communicates. Do not add
  geometric ornament to announce “Bauhaus”. Keep craft and implementation together.
- Preserve the distinction between enclosed identity and bare operational signal.
  A Wada chord may inform a separate exhibition, not random route or alert colours.
- Test direct entry: system identity, page name, position, next action and return.
  Prefer conventional controls when they reduce the work of understanding.
- Verify the complete task with keyboard, readable contrast, reflow, actual
  languages, no-match/error/recovery and reduced motion. Inspect actual pixels.
- Observe an uninstructed real person. Record task failures before taste opinions.
  A clean deterministic audit is a floor, not a claim of human validation.
